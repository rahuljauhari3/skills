# Format checks

These are the most mechanical checks — closest to what an actual PDF/DOCX parser would flag, since most of them are about whether text extracts cleanly and stays legible when a system (not a human) renders it.

## 1. Font size & choice
- Body text: 10-12pt
- Headers/section titles: 14-16pt
- Recommended fonts: Arial, Helvetica, Tahoma, Times New Roman, Verdana, Calibri
- Avoid: Comic Sans, Courier New, Lucinda Console, or any decorative/script font
- A serif font for headings + sans-serif for body (or vice versa) is a nice touch but not required — flag only actual violations of the above, not stylistic taste.

## 2. Margins
Standard margins (roughly 0.5"-1" on all sides). Flag margins so tight the page looks cramped, or so wide the resume looks sparse and under-filled.

## 3. Line spacing
Consistent spacing throughout — flag noticeably inconsistent spacing between sections or within a bullet list (a sign of copy-paste formatting drift).

## 4. Bullet point format
Consistent bullet character and indentation throughout the whole document. Flag mixed bullet styles (e.g., some sections use "•" and others use "-").

## 5. Date format
Consistent date format across every entry (e.g., always "Month Year – Month Year," never mixing that with "MM/YYYY" or "Year-Year" elsewhere in the same document).

## 6. Length
One page for early-career (0-5 years experience or a new grad/MS applicant); up to two pages is acceptable with 5+ years of relevant experience. Flag a one-page resume that's clearly overstuffed (tiny margins/fonts to force a fit) as a length+format combo issue, not just a font issue.
